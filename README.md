# Where-Can-I-Sit-
Where Can I Sit? is a mobile-friendly website that helps people find places to sit and rest while they’re out. It supports older adults, pregnant people, people with disabilities, commuters, and anyone who needs a break.

Users can explore seating on a map or in a list and filter places by shade, backrests, armrests, step-free access, and free seating. Each location includes practical details such as opening hours, access conditions, and nearby facilities. The website also lets people suggest new seats and report changes.

Its warm cream and forest green design creates a calm, welcoming experience with readable text and simple navigation.

The current prototype uses clearly labelled fictional locations and saves contributions only in the user’s browser. Its long-term aim is to become a community-powered guide that makes everyday journeys more comfortable.

Tagline: “A little rest, closer than you think.”
The unique part of “Where Can I Sit?” is its focus on finding a suitable place to rest, with details that ordinary place listings may overlook.
- Comfort-based filters: Find seats with shade, backrests, or armrests.
- Clear access conditions: Know whether seating is free, indoors, or requires a café purchase.
- Specific physical details: Step-free access and seating features help people decide whether a spot meets their needs.
- Community contributions: People can suggest overlooked benches and report damage or changes.
- Rest as the destination: The website centres an everyday need that matters especially to people who cannot comfortably walk or stand for long.
Your strongest project pitch: “We help people plan their journey around places they can rest.”

The current version demonstrates this idea with fictional locations; verified local information would make it useful in everyday life.
